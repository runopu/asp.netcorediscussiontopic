## Strategy

The company page should communicate:

> **We build solutions.**

The personal profile should communicate:

> **I understand these problems and know how to solve them.**

Instead of making every post purely technical, connect technical capabilities to business outcomes.

### Example

**Technical angle:**

> Redis in ASP.NET Core: Make Your Applications Faster

**Client-focused angle:**

> Is Your Business Application Getting Slower as Your Customer Base Grows?

Then explain how SQL optimization, caching, Redis, API optimization, and architecture can contribute to solving the business problem.
